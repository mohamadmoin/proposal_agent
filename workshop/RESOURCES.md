# Learn more: resources for each part of the workshop

All links were checked on 2026-10-25. Free resources come first. The slide numbers refer to `OUTLINE.md`.

## Product thinking (slides 4–7)
- Y Combinator, *How to Get and Evaluate Startup Ideas* (video, 32 min): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Th8JoIan4dg
- YC Startup School (free course): https://www.startupschool.org/
- Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*, on how to talk to users without fooling yourself: https://www.momtestbook.com/

## LLMs, prompting and structured outputs (slides 9–11, 16–18)
- OpenAI, prompt engineering guide: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/prompt-engineering
- Anthropic, prompt engineering overview: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview
- OpenAI, Structured Outputs: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs
- Gemini through the OpenAI-compatible API: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/openai
- Pydantic documentation: https://pydantic.dev/docs/validation/latest/get-started/
- DeepLearning.AI, *ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers* (free short course): https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/chatgpt-prompt-eng

## Research methods and reporting guidelines (slides 12, 29, 34)
- EQUATOR Network, a library of reporting guidelines: https://www.equator-network.org/
- STROBE (observational studies): https://www.strobe-statement.org/
- CONSORT (randomised trials): https://www.consort-spirit.org/
- PRISMA (systematic reviews): https://www.prisma-statement.org/
- OpenEpi, free sample-size and epidemiology calculators: https://www.openepi.com/ (the site can be slow to load)
