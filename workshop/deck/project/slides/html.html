Step 2 · §2.4 · concept: parsing and licences

Parsing reads pages as data; licences decide what you may take

Allowed: the API

PubMed indexes StatPearls chapters. E-utilities returns their summary, section titles and reference list as XML.

Not allowed: scraping

Bookshelf pages answer scripts with a reCAPTCHA; the StatPearls site does not allow AI input. We never bypass either.

Your own copy

Save the chapter in your browser and upload it: BeautifulSoup reads its sections and references.
