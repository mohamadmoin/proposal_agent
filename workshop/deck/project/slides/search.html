These lines are search engineering: find the right papers cheaply

core_query: the study’s subject only — the condition and the population, at most 3 blocks … never prevalence, associated factors or design words

Lanes: core · core AND design filter · core AND last 10 years · plain words · core AND country — 50 results each

TF-IDF over title + title + abstract … reciprocal-rank fusion … keep the top 40

At most 3 requests/s without a key and 9/s with one, at least 110 ms apart

The first line was added after a live run: aim words in the query hid the most similar studies.
