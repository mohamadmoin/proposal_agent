medicine

Input: a study title (Persian or English, required); an optional purpose; an optional study design, or empty to let the app decide; the output language: fa (default) or en … Users: medical students and researchers. The output is a draft; the researcher is the author.

Each input fixes a real weakness

  • Purpose: the 2024 tool only had a title and sent it twice, as “title” and as “purpose”
  • Design is optional: students often don’t know it yet; the app reasons it out (§2.3)
  • Persian by default: the university form is Persian
