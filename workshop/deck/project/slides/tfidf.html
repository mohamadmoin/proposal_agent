3 · The stack · TF-IDF

TF-IDF ranks text by shared rare words, fast and free

TF: how often a word appears in this abstract. IDF: how rare it is across all abstracts. “ferritin” counts, “study” barely does.

Each abstract and the study profile become vectors; the cosine of the angle between them is the similarity.

In this app

  • About 600 records → the best 40
  • Title counted twice, plus the abstract
  • Fused with the rank in each search (papers several searches agree on rise)
  • Costs no model call at all

Learn more: scikit-learn, TF-IDF · Jurafsky & Martin, ch. 6 (free book)

33:00–34:30. TF-IDF is a 50-year-old idea and still useful: a free first filter so the expensive model reads 40 abstracts instead of 600. The old version of this tool used TF-IDF on titles only with a fixed 0.3 threshold; the new one uses titles and abstracts, keeps the top 40 and combines this with the agreement between searches (reciprocal-rank fusion).
