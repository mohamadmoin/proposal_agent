Step 2 · §3 · concept: TF-IDF and rank fusion

TF-IDF ranks text by shared rare words — fast, free, explainable

TF: how often a word appears in this abstract. IDF: how rare it is across all abstracts. “ferritin” counts; “study” barely does.

Each abstract and the study profile become vectors; the cosine of the angle between them is the similarity.

Rank fusion: 1/(60 + rank) summed over the TF-IDF rank and every search lane that found the paper.

In this app

  • 2,435 hits → the best 40
  • Title counted twice, plus the abstract
  • No model call at all
  • The 2024 tool used titles only, with a fixed 0.3 cut-off
