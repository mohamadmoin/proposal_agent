Every principle answers a real failure of the 2024 version: the design reached the writers as one letter (“C” for cohort), the introduction was written from a chapter title instead of its text, failed runs were marked done with empty sections (23 of 82 proposals), and nothing intermediate was kept, so no sentence could be traced.
