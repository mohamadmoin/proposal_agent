Small jobs let small models do expert work

One job per call

Screen five abstracts. Write one paragraph. Never “write my proposal”.

Short inputs

A paragraph call sees one abstract, so findings never mix between papers.

Structured outputs

The answer is data with a fixed shape, not text to parse.

An expert persona

“You are an epidemiologist… follow STROBE, CONSORT, PRISMA… never invent numbers.”
