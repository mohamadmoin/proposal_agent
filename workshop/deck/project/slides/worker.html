39:00–40:30. Why not just run the pipeline inside the web request? It takes 1–2 minutes; browsers and servers give up long before. A queue plus a worker is the standard pattern. We chose the simplest real version (a table and SKIP LOCKED) over Celery to keep the number of moving parts small for a teaching project; the design document records that choice and its trade-off.
