Three small assumptions that quietly lowered quality

“The most similar title is the best chapter”

Showed: an “Iron-Deficiency Anemia (Archived)” chapter was chosen.

Why: StatPearls keeps superseded chapters under almost the same title; text similarity cannot tell old from current.

Fix: a current chapter always wins.

“Percentages are written with %”

Showed: “3.8 percent” in an abstract did not match 0.038, so a correct prevalence became an “assumption”.

Why: journals have house styles: %, percent, per cent; Persian writes درصد.

Fix: all four count as a percentage.

“Keep terms without a Persian equivalent in English” is clear

Showed: “percent”, “greater than or equal to” inside Persian paragraphs.

Why: one model read the rule too widely and copied English fragments.

Fix: the rule now lists what stays English (title, abbreviations, units) with examples.
