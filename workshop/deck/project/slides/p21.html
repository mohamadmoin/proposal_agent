16:00–17:30. One small model call reads the Persian title and returns a structured profile. The core query is a Boolean query: OR joins synonyms inside a block, AND joins the blocks. Every AND you add narrows the result — and that is exactly where our first real mistake happened. The words “the study’s subject only … at most 3 blocks” were NOT in the first version of this prompt. Next slide: why they were added.
