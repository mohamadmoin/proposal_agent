A good build prompt has the shape of a protocol

  • Background: who we are, what to read first
  • Aim: what the app does, for whom
  • Methods: the rules of every step (§2–§4)
  • Procedure: how to work (§5)
  • Outcome measures: how we know it is done (§6)
