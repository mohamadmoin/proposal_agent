19:00–21:00. The questionnaire and templates are in knowledge/questionnaire.md and knowledge/designs.yaml. Each template has five design-specific search topics (reporting-guideline items turned into PubMed searches) and Persian and English instructions for each of the five methods sub-sections. Any other design (qualitative, diagnostic accuracy…) uses a general template, so the app never fails on an unknown design. In our test of six titles, the app chose the right design six times out of six.
