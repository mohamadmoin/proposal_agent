# Methods evidence: the questions answered before any methods text is written

Before writing the methods chapter, one step reads the abstracts of the most similar studies (from the background
search) and the short summaries of the design topics, and answers this checklist. The section writers then get these
notes, not raw abstracts. Everything comes from the given sources. When a source has no answer, write "not reported".

a. **Main outcome**: the main outcome or disease of the study, and how it is defined or diagnosed.
b. **Baseline numbers for the sample size**: prevalence, incidence, means and SDs, proportions in the comparison
   groups, odds or risk ratios, correlations. Give each with its paper token and the sentence it comes from.
c. **Inclusion and exclusion criteria** typically used by similar studies.
d. **Population**: who, where, which setting, which age range.
e. **Variables**: one entry per variable:
   - its role: outcome, exposure or intervention, confounder, background;
   - its type: continuous, binary, categorical or ordinal, time-to-event;
   - how and when it is measured.
f. **Instruments**: questionnaires, scales or tests used by similar studies, and whether a validated Persian version is
   reported.
g. **Statistical analysis**: what similar studies used: descriptive statistics, comparison tests, regression,
   survival, adjustment for confounders.
h. **Intervention details** (trials and quasi-experiments only): what, dose or intensity, duration, comparator.
