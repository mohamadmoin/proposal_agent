# Consistency checklist (before finish, every proposal)

When to read: before your last submission and when the critic reports issues.
How to use: check each line against your aims, decisions, methods and sample size; fix contradictions by submitting
the corrected section again; if an item does not apply, say why in record_decision.

- Title, main aim, design and population say the same thing (period, setting, population).
- Every specific aim has a question, a hypothesis (or "not applicable" for a descriptive aim), the variables it needs,
  and a named statistical test.
- The primary outcome of the sample size is the primary outcome of the aims and of the analysis.
- Outcome wording fits the design (prevalence for one measurement; incidence only with a baseline and follow-up;
  effect only in a controlled design).
- Exclusions do not remove factors you plan to analyse or adjust for; confounders are measured before the outcome.
- Matching (if any) is on named non-exposure variables and the analysis is matched.
- Sampling (cluster, consecutive, census) matches the sample-size corrections (DEFF) and the analysis (cluster-aware).
- Loss to follow-up is attrition (with handling), never an exclusion.
- Trials: fixed dose and duration, a real comparator, ITT primary, no post-randomisation exclusions.
- Consent fits the observation method; ethics part present.
- Timeline: recruitment and follow-up fit the period and the sample size.
- Every number in the text matches the computed sample size and the evidence.
