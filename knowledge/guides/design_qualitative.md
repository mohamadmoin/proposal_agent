# Qualitative study (COREQ / SRQR)

When to read: after choosing a qualitative design.
How to use: there is no formula sample size; describe sampling to saturation and the analysis approach.

Essentials of the protocol
- Approach (phenomenology, grounded theory, qualitative content analysis) and why it fits the question.
- Purposive sampling with criteria; expected range of participants; data saturation rule.
- Data collection: semi-structured interview guide (topics), who interviews, setting, recording and transcription.
- Analysis: method (e.g. Graneheim and Lundman content analysis), coding, software, how themes are derived.
- Trustworthiness: credibility, dependability, confirmability, transferability (Lincoln and Guba) and the concrete
  steps (member checking, peer debriefing, audit trail).
- Reflexivity and ethics: consent for recording, confidentiality, withdrawal.
