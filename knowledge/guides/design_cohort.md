# Cohort study (STROBE)

When to read: after choosing a cohort design (prospective or retrospective), before the sample size and the methods.
How to use: decide each item (record_decision for exposure, outcome, follow-up, confounders) and write it in the
matching sub-section.

Essentials of the protocol
- Exposure groups defined at baseline (who is exposed / unexposed, how it is measured, when).
- Outcome definition, the instrument and its validated cut-off, and WHEN it is measured. Incidence requires that
  participants are free of the outcome at baseline: measure the outcome at baseline (e.g. EPDS in pregnancy for
  postpartum depression) and exclude or adjust for prevalent cases. One measurement only gives prevalence.
- Follow-up: schedule (dates or weeks), duration long enough for the outcome (e.g. recurrence after hernia repair needs
  at least 1–2 years), method (visit, phone, records), and how loss to follow-up is minimised and analysed (it is
  attrition, never an exclusion criterion).
- Retrospective cohorts: source records, period, completeness, how outcomes missing from records are ascertained.
- Confounders measured at baseline, before the outcome (not at the same time as the outcome).
- Exclusions must not remove the high-risk part of an exposure group (selection bias toward no effect); say why each
  exclusion is needed.
- Sample size: two proportions (risk in exposed vs unexposed) from a comparable cohort; allocation ratio from the
  expected exposure frequency; attrition. Rare outcomes need large n: check feasibility.
- Analysis: incidence with 95% CI; RR (log-binomial or modified Poisson) or HR (Kaplan–Meier, Cox) for time-to-event
  outcomes with variable follow-up; adjusted models with the named confounders; censoring and missing data.

Pitfalls seen in reviews
- "Incidence" with a single outcome measurement and no baseline.
- Adjusting for a factor that an exclusion criterion already removed.
- Logistic regression for recurrence with variable follow-up (use survival methods).
- Loss to follow-up listed as an exclusion criterion.
