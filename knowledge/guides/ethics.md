# Ethics (every proposal)

When to read: before submitting the methods. Every proposal has an ethics part (as its own paragraph in the conduct
sub-section and in the ethics field).
How to use: write each applicable item concretely for this study; do not copy generic text that contradicts the
methods.

Items
- Approval by the university's research ethics committee before data collection, with the national ethics code
  (IR.xxx.REC…) to be obtained; trials registered in IRCT before enrolment.
- Informed consent: written, voluntary, the right to withdraw without consequence; for minors, guardian consent plus
  assent; for records-only (retrospective) studies, the committee's waiver of consent and permission from the hospital.
- Confidentiality: coded data, no names in the analysis files, secure storage, access limited to the team; results
  reported only in aggregate.
- Benefit and harm: no extra cost to participants; any blood sampling or intervention risks stated and managed;
  abnormal results (e.g. deficiency, positive depression screening) referred for care.
- Vulnerable groups (pregnant women, children, patients): extra protections stated.
- Observation studies: overt observation with consent is the default; covert observation only with explicit ethical
  justification and committee approval, and it then cannot rely on prior individual consent; debriefing.
- Declaration of Helsinki and national guidelines followed; conflicts of interest declared.
